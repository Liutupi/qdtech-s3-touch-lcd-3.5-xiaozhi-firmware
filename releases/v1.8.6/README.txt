QDTech ESP32-S3 Touch LCD 3.5 - v1.8.6

Contents
========

- qdtech-s3-touch-lcd-3.5-v1.8.6-app.bin
  OTA application image. Use this for normal OTA updates.

- qdtech-s3-touch-lcd-3.5-v1.8.6-full.bin
  Complete recovery image. Flash at offset 0x0 only for recovery.
  It may clear local Wi-Fi provisioning state.

- qdtech-s3-touch-lcd-3.5-v1.8.6-firmware.zip
  Convenience archive containing the app and complete recovery images.

- qdtech-s3-touch-lcd-3.5-v1.8.6-sdcard.zip
  Extract to the root of a FAT-formatted SD card. It includes the Calendar
  Zodiac assets and radio.json with 47 verified direct-MP3 stations.

v1.8.6 adds the experimental QDTech Radio directory with five Chinese,
non-empty groups and direct station selection. The Guangdong group includes
Music FM, City Voice, Pearl River Voice, Culture & Sports, Southern Life,
Traffic Voice and local stations. Wi-Fi, weather, touch, BMI270, NVS and
the normal radio path are retained.
